## My first post

Hello, world! I have wanted to create a little website for my projects for a long time but never really had the time to do so. Now, and with the help of agentic AI, I finally set it up and am happy to share some of the stuff that I am doing and I am interested in.  
  
I am currently still in my [Master's in Environmental System Sciences](https://www.uni-graz.at/en/studies/master-programmes/environmental-systems-sciences-economics/) at the University of Graz whilest working at the [Joanneum Research LIFE Institue](https://www.joanneum.at/life/en/) broadly on topics concerning the green transition. Broadly means  doing research on environmental awareness, the decarbonization of the European Building Stock to the establishment of flexibility options in the grid - so a wide array of different topics. As a student research assistent I mainly help with background research and coding task - if necessary.  
  
My Master's is also pretty diverse. I learned varying methods from System Dynamics, the usage of Networks and Agent-Based models to the more traditional economic methods of Computable General Equilibrium Modelling as well as Econometrics. The former I do not plan to use anymore whereas the latter interests me more. The mixture of methods gives me a broad overview of modern quantitative modelling but what has interested me by far the most so far is Complex Systems Analysis and Complexity Science as a whole. I currently think it is the framework to understand and anlayze the modern world. Maybe this belief will change or turn out to be not as important in the future but at the moment this is my state of mind. That being said, I do not have a lot of time to study complexity science as this is whether a priority in my Master's program nor at my position as a research assistent. So, this website will also be an outlet for the stuff that I am interest in but can not really work on in my main occupations - unfortunately.
